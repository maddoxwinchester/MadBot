Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Overview

MadBot Extensions is a one-person project building lightweight Chrome extensions, including Roblox Trade Finder. This page explains what data these extensions handle and what they don't.

What we don't do

No analytics, no tracking pixels or scripts, no ad networks, and no selling or sharing of your data with anyone. There's no company behind this and no server that collects your personal information — there isn't a backend at all beyond the extension itself.

Roblox Trade Finder

Account & inventory data. The extension reads public Roblox data (your inventory, trade info, friends/followers, account age) directly from Roblox's own public web APIs, using your browser's existing logged-in Roblox session — the same way the roblox.com site itself works. It also reads public item values from Rolimons' public API. Both happen directly between your browser and Roblox/Rolimons; nothing passes through a server we operate.

Local storage. Search settings, selected items, and match history are stored locally on your device using Chrome's own chrome.storage.local. We have no access to it, and it never leaves your device except to Roblox/Rolimons as described above.

The debugger permission. Used only on Roblox's own trade page, only for the Pro auto-fill feature, to send real click and type events that fill in the trade offer for you. This is a technical requirement because Roblox's trade page ignores script-simulated clicks. It attaches and detaches automatically around each action (you'll see Chrome's own "being debugged" banner while it's active), and is never used to read, log, capture, or transmit anything from the page.

Payments

Pro purchases are handled entirely by ExtensionPay, which uses Stripe for payment processing. We never see or store your card details. ExtensionPay/Stripe may collect your email address to associate a purchase with your license — see ExtensionPay's privacy policy and Stripe's privacy policy for how they handle that.

Changes
