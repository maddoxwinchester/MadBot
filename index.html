About

MadBot Extensions is a small, one-person project, built and maintained directly, no company behind it. Everything here is free to install and use. A couple offer an optional Pro tier for extra features, handled through ExtensionPay right inside the extension itself.

Questions

Do these track or sell any data?

No. No analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party data sharing. Whatever an extension needs to store (like watch progress) stays in Chrome's own local or sync storage.

Why do some need "all sites" access?

Only extensions whose whole job is watching every page for something (a video player, highlighted text) request that. It's used to detect that one thing, not to read or store anything else on the page.

How do I get one?

Nothing's published yet. All five extensions are built and working, they're just not live anywhere to install. Once one ships, any Pro tier runs through ExtensionPay, right inside the extension itself.
